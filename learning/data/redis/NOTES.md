# Redis Notes

Working notes for the teaching session. Not linked from `README.md`.

## Preferences

- Interview recorded in #38. The outcome was not narrowed: both spotting misuse in an existing system and designing correct usage from scratch.
- Distributed Redis, meaning Cluster and Sentinel, was kept at the level of trade-offs rather than operations. The learner asked to understand the choice, not to run the topology, so a lesson that drifts into operating a cluster has gone past what was asked for.
- The comparison with `data/postgres` was asked for on both halves: the durability guarantees and when to choose one over the other. Keep both, and link rather than restate what that workspace owns.
