# C# Notes

Working notes for the teaching session. Not linked from `README.md`.

## Preferences

- **This is a standalone zero-to-senior course, not a delta over Java. Requested explicitly, and do not reintroduce the prerequisite.** The workspace was first written as one of only two in the repository framed as "assumes professional Java background", and that framing was removed on instruction (see #43). The Java and Kotlin contrast survives only as a habit C# punishes, and the async/virtual-threads and LINQ/Stream-API comparisons moved to the final judgment stage as optional rather than assumed.
- Interview recorded in #43. The outcome was the same "own X on a team" shape as the other language workspaces, and the context was narrowed on purpose: ASP.NET Core backend rather than general .NET, with Unity and game development explicitly out.
- That narrowing is the learner's own, not an editorial guess. It is the one workspace where the interview cut scope rather than pulling it in, so do not widen the arc back out to .NET at large.

## On the arc

The seven stages are public, in `README.md`. What belongs here is the caveat on them: the 21 lessons written so far were produced upfront across successive runs, not one per interactive session, and `learning-records/` is empty. So nothing in this workspace has been calibrated against a demonstrated answer, and every practice item's difficulty is guessed.

The arc is also incomplete. Stages 1 to 4 are written (lessons 0001 to 0021); stages 5 to 7, meaning testing and build, shipping the service, and judgment, are designed in the arc table but not yet written.
